drake.glb (all drake mounts: Ember, Frost, Shadow and Nether Drake)

Based on "Animated Dragon Three Motion Loops" by LasquetiSpice
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-dragon-three-motion-loops-eca98cf6cd084c1596cecf716e110c29
and its run cycle from "Animated Dragon Runs Slow Motion Loop" by LasquetiSpice
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-dragon-runs-slow-motion-loop-840128a1f87a41698464a135627797df
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the two files were combined into one (same rig), one wingbeat and one stride were cut from the loops,
the material was converted to metal/roughness and the textures were resized to 1024 px (WebP).
In the game each drake is recoloured in a shader (palette, glowing creases, glowing wing membranes).
